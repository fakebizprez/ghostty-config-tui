# Icon assets

The PNGs in this folder are downscaled (160px) copies of images from
`macos/Assets.xcassets` in [ghostty-org/ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty),
collected by [zerebos/ghostty-config](https://github.com/zerebos/ghostty-config).
